# Brief 06: Days-per-Week Mapping & Combining Splits

**Output file:** `research/raw/06-days-per-week.md`
**Output format:** follow `research/briefs/00-output-contract.md` exactly. Read it first.
**Context:** `research/01-domain-map.md` shows how this topic fits the whole wiki. Read it.

## Scope — research all of the following
- For each of 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7 training days/week: recommended split options (ranked), per-muscle frequency achieved, sets per session needed to hit weekly volume targets, rest-day layout across a calendar week, and trade-offs.
- How the mapping changes by goal: strength, hypertrophy, general fitness, fat loss, hybrid (strength + cardio).
- How to combine splits (e.g. 5 days = upper/lower + PPL; 4 days = upper/lower or PHUL; 6 days = PPL x2 or ULPPL+) and the rules for combining so recovery isn't violated (e.g. avoid heavy legs on consecutive days).
- Where to put cardio/conditioning days relative to lifting days at each day count.
- Minimum effective training: what can 1–2 days/week achieve (evidence on low-frequency training).
- Maximum: is 6–7 days sensible, for whom, and how to manage fatigue.
- Produce a weekly calendar table (Mon–Sun) for each day count, with at least two options each.
