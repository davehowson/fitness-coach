# Brief 02: Resistance Training Variables & Dose-Response

**Output file:** `research/raw/02-training-variables.md`
**Output format:** follow `research/briefs/00-output-contract.md` exactly. Read it first.
**Context:** `research/01-domain-map.md` shows how this topic fits the whole wiki. Read it.

## Scope — research all of the following
- Volume: sets per muscle per week, dose-response meta-analyses (e.g. Schoenfeld 2017, Pelland 2024 or newer), volume landmarks (MV, MEV, MAV, MRV), how to count sets (direct vs. indirect / fractional).
- Intensity: %1RM, RPE, RIR; load ranges for strength vs. hypertrophy; proximity to failure research (e.g. Refalo, Robinson meta-analyses).
- Frequency: per-muscle frequency, volume-equated vs. not; how frequency matters for strength vs. hypertrophy.
- Rep ranges and the "repetition continuum" — and current view that hypertrophy occurs across wide load ranges.
- Rest intervals between sets (Schoenfeld, Singer meta-analyses).
- Tempo / repetition duration; range of motion and lengthened-partials research.
- Exercise selection: compound vs. isolation, movement-pattern categories, free weights vs. machines, need for exercise variation.
- Exercise order within a session.
- Advanced techniques: supersets, drop sets, rest-pause, cluster sets, myo-reps — effects and efficiency.
- Give default numeric tables per goal (strength, hypertrophy, endurance, power) for every variable.
