# Brief 03: Training Goals & Workout Types

**Output file:** `research/raw/03-goals-and-types.md`
**Output format:** follow `research/briefs/00-output-contract.md` exactly. Read it first.
**Context:** `research/01-domain-map.md` shows how this topic fits the whole wiki. Read it.

## Scope — research all of the following
- Catalog workout goals/types: maximal strength, hypertrophy, power/explosiveness, muscular endurance, cardiovascular endurance, general fitness/health, fat loss / body recomposition, mobility/flexibility, athletic/sport performance, functional fitness, hybrid.
- For each: definition, primary adaptations, how the program looks (variables, modalities, session structure), typical weekly structure, what to prioritize and what to de-emphasize.
- How goals interact: compatible vs. competing goals; how to blend a primary and secondary goal.
- Training for fat loss: role of resistance training vs. cardio vs. diet (keep nutrition brief — only as it affects program design).
- Training for longevity/health in the general population.
- Distinguish training modalities: free weights, machines, bodyweight/calisthenics, kettlebells, plyometrics, Olympic lifting, CrossFit-style metcons, circuits.
