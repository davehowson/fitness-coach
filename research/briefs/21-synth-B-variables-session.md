## Page scope
- `03-variables.md`: every resistance-training dial (volume and fractional set counting, per-session cap, intensity/load, RIR/RPE, frequency, rep ranges, rest, tempo, ROM, exercise selection principles, order, advanced techniques). Each has a definition, the canonical default, ranges per goal (strength / hypertrophy / power / muscular endurance / general fitness), and evidence. End with one master table: goal × variable.
- `05-session-design.md`: building one session: block order, warm-up protocol (RAMP, ramp sets with concrete set/rep/% ladders), how many exercises, time budgets for 30/45/60/75/90 min (what fits, what to cut first), supersets and pairing rules, pattern balance within a session, cool-down, and 5+ worked session templates with exercise categories, sets, reps, RIR and rest. Link to `12-exercise-library.md` for exercise choice.
