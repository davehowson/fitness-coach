**Output file:** `research/raw/08-conditioning.md`
**Output format:** follow `research/briefs/00-output-contract.md` exactly. Read it first.
**Context:** `research/01-domain-map.md` shows how this topic fits the whole wiki. Read it.

## Scope — research all of the following
- Energy systems and the adaptations from aerobic vs. anaerobic training.
- Intensity zones: heart-rate zones, %HRmax, %HRR, RPE, talk test, lactate/ventilatory thresholds; zone 2 definition and evidence.
- Types: LISS / steady-state, tempo/threshold, VO2max intervals, HIIT, SIT, fartlek, circuits/metcons, rucking, rowing, cycling, running, swimming.
- Training intensity distribution: polarized vs. pyramidal vs. threshold; evidence for recreational vs. trained athletes.
- Weekly dose guidelines for health, general fitness, fat loss, endurance performance.
- Session structure for cardio sessions: warm-up, main set, cool-down; concrete interval protocols with work:rest ratios.
- Progression for cardio: volume vs intensity, the 10% rule (and its evidence).
- Cardio for lifters who mainly want strength/hypertrophy: how much, what type, minimal interference.
- Provide example weekly cardio plans for 2, 3, 4, 5 sessions/week.
