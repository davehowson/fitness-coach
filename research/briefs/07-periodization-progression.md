# Brief 07: Periodization & Progression

**Output file:** `research/raw/07-periodization-progression.md`
**Output format:** follow `research/briefs/00-output-contract.md` exactly. Read it first.
**Context:** `research/01-domain-map.md` shows how this topic fits the whole wiki. Read it.

## Scope — research all of the following
- Progression models: linear progression, double progression, rep-range progression, load/rep/set progression, RPE/RIR autoregulation, percentage-based, AMRAP-driven (e.g. 5/3/1), top set + back-off sets.
- Periodization models: linear/traditional, block, daily undulating (DUP), weekly undulating, conjugate, reverse linear. Meta-analytic evidence on periodized vs. non-periodized and between models.
- Mesocycle and macrocycle structure: typical lengths, volume ramping within a mesocycle.
- Deloads: when, how often, how (volume vs. intensity reduction), evidence and practitioner consensus.
- How progression differs for beginner, intermediate, advanced lifters; definitions of each level.
- Plateaus: how to detect and what to change.
- Exercise rotation across mesocycles.
- Tracking: what to log and how to use it.
- Provide concrete progression schemes with numbers (e.g. "add 2.5 kg upper / 5 kg lower per session until…").
