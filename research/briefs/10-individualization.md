# Brief 10: Individualization & Special Populations

**Output file:** `research/raw/10-individualization.md`
**Output format:** follow `research/briefs/00-output-contract.md` exactly. Read it first.
**Context:** `research/01-domain-map.md` shows how this topic fits the whole wiki. Read it.

## Scope — research all of the following
- Training-experience levels: how to classify beginner / intermediate / advanced, and how each changes volume, complexity, progression, exercise selection.
- Older adults (50+, 65+): sarcopenia, power training, balance, guidelines (ACSM, NSCA position stand on older adults).
- Women: evidence on sex differences in training response, fatigue resistance, menstrual cycle (evidence on cycle-based training), pregnancy/postpartum (brief, safety-oriented).
- Youth/adolescents (brief, NSCA youth position).
- Time-constrained individuals: minimum effective dose, efficient session designs.
- Home / minimal equipment / bodyweight: how to achieve overload without heavy load.
- Injuries and limitations: general principles of working around pain, exercise substitution logic (by movement pattern), when to refer to a professional. Do not give medical treatment advice.
- Returning after a layoff / detraining and retraining (muscle memory).
- Recovery capacity factors: sleep, stress, nutrition (only as it affects program design), and how to adjust volume.
- Overweight/obese beginners and sedentary individuals: safe starting points.
- Produce an exercise-substitution matrix by movement pattern × equipment level.

## Done when
- `research/raw/10-individualization.md` exists with every section from the output contract.
- Every scope bullet above is addressed (or explicitly marked as not findable, with why).
- 12+ distinct sources with URLs; key findings carry evidence grades.
