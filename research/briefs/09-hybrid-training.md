**Output file:** `research/raw/09-hybrid-training.md`
**Output format:** follow `research/briefs/00-output-contract.md` exactly. Read it first.
**Context:** `research/01-domain-map.md` shows how this topic fits the whole wiki. Read it.

## Scope — research all of the following
- The concurrent training "interference effect": original findings (Hickson 1980), modern meta-analyses (e.g. Wilson 2012, Schumann 2022), effects on strength vs. hypertrophy vs. power; who is affected.
- Moderators: modality (cycling vs running), intensity, volume, frequency, training status, sex.
- Sequencing: same-session order (strength first vs. endurance first), same-day separation and recovery gap (hours), separate days. Evidence-based rules.
- How to structure a hybrid week at 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7 days with different priorities (strength-primary, endurance-primary, equal).
- Hybrid athlete approaches (e.g. Nick Bare, Fergus Crawley, Alex Viada's "The Hybrid Athlete"), HYROX training structure, CrossFit/functional fitness programming principles.
- Periodizing hybrid training: blocks emphasizing one quality, concurrent vs. sequential/block approaches.
- Recovery and fatigue management specific to hybrid training.
- Provide example weekly hybrid templates with a calendar table.
