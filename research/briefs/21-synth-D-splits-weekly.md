## Page scope
- `06-splits.md`: catalog of every split (full body, upper/lower, PPL, PPL×2, bro split, Arnold, torso/limbs, anterior/posterior, push/pull, PHUL, PHAT, ULPPL and other hybrids, specialization, powerlifting-style). Per split: day layout, muscles per day, per-muscle frequency, sessions/week, sets per session, pros, cons, best fit, variants. A master comparison table. Evidence on splits. Rolling vs fixed-calendar schedules and how to handle missed days.
- `07-weekly-planning.md`: the days-per-week playbook. For EACH of 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 days: ranked split options, per-muscle frequency achieved, sets per muscle per session needed for the weekly target (show the arithmetic from canonical defaults), Mon–Sun calendar tables (≥2 options each), how it changes by goal (strength, hypertrophy, general fitness, fat loss, hybrid), where cardio goes. Rules for combining splits (e.g. U/L + PPL) and recovery spacing (no heavy same-pattern on consecutive days). Link to `10-hybrid.md` for detailed hybrid weeks.
