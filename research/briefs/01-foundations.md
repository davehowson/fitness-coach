# Brief 01: Foundations: What Makes a Good Training Program

**Output file:** `research/raw/01-foundations.md`
**Output format:** follow `research/briefs/00-output-contract.md` exactly. Read it first.
**Context:** `research/01-domain-map.md` shows how this topic fits the whole wiki. Read it.

## Scope — research all of the following
- Universal training principles: specificity, progressive overload, SRA (stimulus-recovery-adaptation), individualization, variation, fatigue management, reversibility, minimum effective dose. Define each and how it shows up in a plan.
- What experts and position stands (ACSM, NSCA, WHO physical activity guidelines) say defines an effective program.
- The role of adherence, enjoyment and sustainability vs. "optimal" programming — evidence on this.
- What information must be collected from a person before writing a plan (intake / needs analysis). Produce a concrete intake checklist.
- A generic, top-down process coaches use to design a program (needs analysis → goal → structure → variables → progression → review).
- Criteria to judge whether a finished plan is good: produce a quality-control checklist.
- Minimum health guidelines (WHO/ACSM weekly activity minimums for strength and cardio) as a baseline floor.
