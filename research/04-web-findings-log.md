## 2026-10-10: Do 3 gym days suffice? Split choice when the user rejects full body

**Question:** With 3 gym days (+1 run), is a 4th lifting day needed? Which split fits 3 days if the person dislikes full body?

**Sources**
- Schoenfeld BJ et al. Effects of Resistance Training Frequency on Measures of Muscle Hypertrophy: Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. 2016. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27102172/
- Schoenfeld BJ et al. Resistance training frequency and skeletal muscle hypertrophy: a review of available evidence. 2018. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30236847/
- Henselmans M. Training frequency meta-analysis review (critique of volume-equating). https://mennohenselmans.com/training-frequency-2018-meta-analysis-review/
- Practitioner hybrid guide (commercial, not peer-reviewed): https://www.findyouredge.app/news/how-many-days-run-and-lift

**Findings**
1. Hypertrophy frequency: 2016 meta (10 studies) favoured ≥2×/wk (ES 0.49 vs 0.30) but was not volume-equated. 2018 review and 2019 meta (25 studies) found no significant frequency effect when weekly volume is equal. Henselmans: many "volume-equated" studies still gave the higher-frequency group more total work. Net: frequency is mainly a tool to fit more quality sets, not an independent driver. (Moderate/Strong; already in wiki 06/07.)
2. No head-to-head trial of full body vs upper/lower vs PPL found. Differences are per-muscle frequency only: FB ≈3×, U/L (3 d, alternating) ≈1.5×, PPL once = 1×. (Gap confirmed.)
3. Hybrid lifting + running: practitioner sources give 3 lifting days as the common recommendation / "minimum effective dose", 2 lifting days workable if full body and spaced from hard runs. 6 h gap figure is cited without a specific study. (Practitioner only.)
4. A 4th lifting day is NOT required for a 3-day-lifter. The wiki's "4 d → upper/lower" is a days-to-split default for people who have 4 days; it is not a reason to add a day.

**Wiki pages updated:** `wiki/06-splits.md` (default map + evidence note), `wiki/07-weekly-planning.md` (3-day option E + decision rule), `wiki/10-hybrid.md` (practitioner 3-lift-day note).

**What it overturned:** a coach recommended adding a 4th gym day (U/L x2) to a 3-day user who rejected full body. Correct move: stay at the user's stated days, use alternating U/L (or L/U/L), and explain the frequency trade-off; never add days the user did not offer.
