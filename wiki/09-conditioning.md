# Conditioning (Cardio & Energy-System Training)

> Purpose: choose cardio type, intensity, weekly dose, session structure and progression for a given goal, including lifters adding cardio.
> Use when: any plan includes aerobic/conditioning work (health, fat loss, endurance, general fitness, lifters adding cardio).
> Depends on: [principles](01-principles.md), [goals](04-goals.md), [weekly planning](07-weekly-planning.md), [progression](08-progression.md). Concurrent strength + endurance sequencing/templates live in [hybrid training](10-hybrid.md).

## Key rules (TL;DR)

1. **Health floor:** 150–300 min/wk moderate OR 75–150 min/wk vigorous aerobic (1 vigorous min ≈ 2 moderate) + muscle strengthening of all major groups ≥2 d/wk. Older adults: multicomponent (balance + strength) ≥3 d/wk (WHO 2020; the US PAG gives no day count). `Strong` [1][2][17]
2. **Default distribution:** mostly easy (talk test / RPE 3–4) + 1–2 hard sessions/wk. `Moderate` [3][4]
3. **~80/20 polarized "edge"** (Oliveira 2024, SMD 0.24 VO2peak) applies only to **highly trained athletes in <12-week interventions**; time-trial, time-to-exhaustion and VT2-power outcomes were null. For recreational people, any sensible distribution works; adherence dominates. `Moderate`, narrow [4]
4. **HIIT/SIT vs MICT:** similar or slightly better VO2max in less time. **Fat loss: small and inconsistent difference** (Wewege 2017: none; Viana 2019: ~28.5% more absolute fat-mass loss with intervals). The real HIIT advantage is time-efficiency. `Moderate`/`Contested` [5][9][10]
5. **Wewege caveat:** in that meta-analysis running HIIT and running MICT reduced fat mass; **cycling did not**. Do not promise fat loss from cycling alone. `Moderate` [9]
6. **Zone 2** = good low-fatigue volume; **not** shown to be uniquely superior. Keep ≥1 harder session if VO2max matters. `Contested` [11]
7. **Field intensity tools:** talk test + RPE first; %HRmax / %HRR bands are imprecise defaults. `Moderate` (talk test, validated mainly in cardiac patients) [12]
8. **10% weekly-increase rule is a soft ceiling only** (Buist 2008 RCT: no injury benefit). Still avoid big single-week jumps. `Moderate` [13]
9. **Lifters:** 2–3 cardio sessions/wk is safe; prefer cycling / rowing / incline walking; keep hard intervals away from heavy leg days. `Moderate` [15]
10. **Order of build:** easy volume first (4–8 wk), then add one interval session. Never raise volume and intensity in the same week. `Practitioner`
11. Chest pain, fainting or unusual breathlessness → stop and refer to a clinician; no coaching around it.

## Energy systems

All three systems run simultaneously; training targets the limiter. `Practitioner` (textbook).

| System | Fuel | Dominates | Typical training |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phosphagen (ATP-PCr) | creatine phosphate | 0–~10 s | max sprints, 5–10 s reps, long rest |
| Glycolytic | glucose/glycogen | ~10 s–2 min | 30 s–2 min hard reps, SIT, metcons |
| Oxidative (aerobic) | carbs + fat | >2 min, dominant by ~3 min | steady state, tempo, VO2max intervals |

Aerobic work → stroke volume, capillary density, mitochondria, fat oxidation, lower HR at a given speed. Sprint/anaerobic work → buffering, glycolytic capacity, power; SIT also raises VO2max in novices [8].

## Intensity zones and field tools

Anchors: VT1/LT1 (~2 mmol/L) = upper edge of "easy"; VT2/LT2 = upper edge of "sustainable hard"; VO2max = ceiling. Seiler 3-zone model [3]. **Naming trap:** popular "Zone 2" = Seiler 3-zone **Zone 1** (below VT1). This wiki uses Z1/Z2/Z3 in the Seiler sense and says "easy / Zone 2 (popular)" when needed.

| Zone | %HRmax | %HRR | RPE (0–10) | Talk test | Lactate | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Z1 easy ("Zone 2" popular) | ~60–75 | ~40–60 | 3–4 | full sentences | <2 mM | base, recovery, long sessions |
| Z2 tempo/threshold | ~76–88 | ~60–80 | 5–7 | short phrases | 2–4 mM | tempo, threshold intervals |
| Z3 hard (VO2max) | ~88–95+ | ~80–95 | 8–9 | single words | >4 mM | VO2max intervals |
| Sprint | HR lags; ignore | — | 10 | none | — | all-out ≤30 s |

Caveats `Practitioner`/`Moderate`:
- %HR bands are defaults. HRmax = measured, or 208 − 0.7 × age; do not use 220 − age as exact. Individual VT1 can sit anywhere ~55–80% HRmax. Calibrate with talk test/RPE [12].
- Talk test: last stage where speech is comfortable ≈ VT1 (10 studies, n=414, mostly cardiac patients; one study analysed reliability properly; protocol not standardised) [12].
- HR lags in short intervals and drifts upward in heat/dehydration/long sessions; use RPE for ≤2-min efforts.
- No HR monitor → talk test: full sentences = Z1, short phrases = tempo, single words = interval.

## Modalities

| Modality | Pros | Cons / notes |
|---|---|---|
| Walking / incline walking | lowest impact, easy to add daily | low ceiling for fitness gains unless incline/ruck |
| Cycling / bike | low impact, easy to dose | fat-loss benefit not seen in Wewege cycling subgroup [9]; interference vs running is `Contested` (see [hybrid](10-hybrid.md)) |
| Running | highest specificity for runners, time-efficient | highest impact; progress volume carefully |
| Rowing / ski | full body, low impact | back load at high intensity; technique-limited |
| Swimming | non-weight-bearing | technique-limited |
| Rucking | walking with pack; pack ~22% BM raised HR from ~71% to ~83% HRmax in soldiers (secondary) [18] | start ≤10% BM (≤10 lb / 5 kg), 30 min; add ~5% BM or 10 min every 1–2 wk; speed raises cost more than load; no controlled trials vs other cardio `Practitioner` |
| Sled / assault bike | mostly concentric, low soreness | `Practitioner` |

## Intensity distribution

| Population | Target easy : hard | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| Beginner / sedentary | 90–100% easy; add 1 interval session after 6–8 wk | Practitioner |
| Recreational | ~80 : 20 (hard = tempo + intervals), 1–2 hard sessions/wk | Moderate/Practitioner [3][4] |
| Trained endurance athlete | ~75–80% Z1, ~5% Z2, ~15–20% Z3 (polarized) or pyramidal; both work | Moderate [3][4] |

Oliveira 2024 qualifiers (17 studies, n=437): polarized > other distributions for VO2peak (SMD 0.24, 95% CI 0.01–0.48, I²=0%, 11 studies); edge appeared **only in <12-week interventions (SMD 0.40) and highly trained athletes (SMD 0.46)**; time trial (−0.01), time to exhaustion (0.30, n.s.) and speed/power at VT2 (0.04) null; no recreational-population data [4]. Seiler: the ~80/20 split is descriptive of elite athletes; intensification studies in trained athletes show no convincing benefit of more HIT [3].

## Weekly dose by goal

| Goal | Aerobic min/wk | Hard sessions | Easy sessions | Grade / source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Health minimum | 150–300 moderate (or 75–150 vigorous) | 0–1 | 3–5 | Strong [1][17] |
| General fitness / VO2max | 150–240 | 1–2 | 2–3 | Moderate [7][3] |
| Fat loss (adjunct to diet) | 200–300+ incl. daily steps | 0–2 | 3–5 | Practitioner for upper range; ACSM: >250 min/wk linked to clinically significant loss [19] |
| Endurance performance (recreational) | 240–420 | 1–2 hard + 0–1 tempo | 3–5 | Moderate/Practitioner [3][4] |
| Lifter, strength/size priority | 90–150 (2–3 sessions) | 0–1 | 2 | Moderate [15] |
| Lifter, minimum effective | 60–90 (2 × 30–45) | 0–1 short | 1–2 | Practitioner |

Fat loss is set by energy balance; cardio is a tool to raise expenditure (steps count). Resistance training is the non-negotiable part of a deficit (see [goals](04-goals.md)).

## Session structure

| Block | Duration | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Warm-up | 5–10 min (10–15 before intervals) | easy Z1 build + 2–3 × 20–30 s pickups |
| Main set | per protocol | |
| Cool-down | 5–10 min | easy Z1 until HR <~110 / RPE ≤2 |

`Practitioner`. Cardio >60 min or intervals right after weights: fuel carbohydrate, warm up, trim next-day leg volume.

## Protocol library

Hard-rep intensity: "H" = RPE 8–9 / 85–95% HRmax unless noted. Grades per row.

| Protocol | Work | Recovery | Reps / volume | Freq | Notes / grade |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Steady / LISS (Z1) | 30–90 min | — | — | 1–4×/wk | Beginner entry: 3 × 20–30 min. Practitioner/Moderate [1][3] |
| Tempo | 20–40 min at Z2 ("comfortably hard") | — | — | 0–1×/wk | Practitioner |
| Threshold intervals | 3–10 min near LT2 | 1–2 min jog (work:rest 4:1–1:1) | 20–40 min total work | 0–1×/wk | Practitioner |
| **Norwegian 4×4** | 4 min at 85–95% HRmax (RPE 8) | 3 min active at 60–70% | 4 (add a 5th later) | 1–2×/wk | Helgerud 2007: 4×4 at 90–95% HRmax, 3 min at 70%, 3 d/wk × 8 wk, VO2max +7.2% (n=40 men, single RCT). Moderate [7] |
| **15/15** | 15 s hard | 15 s easy (1:1) | 10–20 min | 1×/wk | Helgerud 15/15: +5.5% VO2max. Moderate [7] |
| **30/30** | 30 s hard | 30 s easy (1:1) | 8–20 reps (10–20 min) | 1×/wk | e.g. 8×30/30 for time-poor. Moderate/Practitioner [14] |
| **10×1** | 1 min hard (RPE 8–9) | 1 min easy | 10 | 1×/wk | Intermediate between 30/30 and long intervals; **author-derived, not in sources** `Practitioner` |
| Long intervals | 2–6 min | 1:1–1:1.5 | 3–6 | 1×/wk | Practitioner [14] |
| Short intervals | 5–20 s (sprint-ish) | ≥1:4 | 8–20 | 0–1×/wk | Practitioner [14] |
| **SIT (Wingate-style)** | 30 s all-out | ~4 min easy | 4–6 (2–3 for beginners) | 1–2×/wk | Gist 2014: vs no-exercise d=0.69, vs endurance-training controls d=0.04 (n.s.): time-efficient, not better than endurance work, unpleasant. Protocol detail is Practitioner. Moderate [8] |
| Fartlek | surges 30 s–3 min | by feel | 20–40 min | 0–1×/wk | Practitioner |
| Circuit / metcon | 20–60 s stations | 1:1–1:2 | 15–30 min | 0–2×/wk | Practitioner |
| Rucking | 20–60 min walk | — | pack 10–20% BM (start ≤10%) | 1–3×/wk | see Modalities [18] |

HIIT/SIT evidence: Milanović 2015 (28 trials, n=723, healthy adults): HIT vs endurance +1.2 ±0.9 mL/kg/min "possibly small" benefit; larger with older subjects, longer intervals, longer interventions [5]. Weston 2014 (patients with cardiometabolic disease, n=273): HIIT > MICT by 3.03 mL/kg/min (~9%) [6]. Intervals are not required for health; they are the efficient route to VO2max.

## Progression

| Variable | Rule | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly volume, running / high impact | +5–10% typical (soft ceiling); up to ~20% OK for healthy novices; avoid >30% jumps; deload every 3–4 wk (−20–30%) | Contested/Practitioner [13] |
| Low-impact (bike/row/swim) | can add faster (10–20%/wk) | Practitioner |
| Intensity | add 1 hard session OR extend (4×4 → 5×4 → 4×5) OR shorten rest; not alongside a volume jump | Practitioner |
| Order | easy base 4–8 wk → then intensity | Practitioner |
| Beginner path | 3 × 20–30 min Z1 → 150 min/wk over 6–8 wk → add intervals | Practitioner [1] |

The 10% rule: GRONORUN RCT (n=532 novice runners) graded 13-week plan vs standard 8-week plan: injury 20.8% vs 20.3% (p=0.90). The graded plan was also longer, so duration differed [13]. The ">30% weekly jump = more injury" claim comes from a secondary source and is unverified; treat as a Practitioner caution.

Cross-link: general progression logic and deloads in [progression](08-progression.md).

## Cardio for lifters

- Dose: 2–3 sessions/wk, 20–45 min, mostly low-impact (bike, incline walk, row); ≤1 interval session/wk. Negligible expected effect on strength or size (Schumann 2022: max strength SMD −0.06, hypertrophy −0.01) [15]. Male lower-body strength is more affected (Huiberts SMD −0.43; female data sparse) [16].
- Keep hard intervals ≥24 h from heavy leg sessions and away from them in the week's layout (see [hybrid](10-hybrid.md) for sequencing).
- Short post-lift easy bike/walk (10–15 min) is fine.
- Explosive-strength/sprint goals: cardio is the thing to minimise (SMD −0.28, mainly same session / <3 h gap) [15].
- Cut low-value cardio before cutting lifting.

## Example weeks (cardio add-on to resistance training; E = easy Z1, H = hard interval)

| Sessions/wk | Total | Layout |
|---|---|---|
| 2 (≈60–90 min) | 60–90 | A: 30–45 min E. B: H (4×4 or 8×30/30) incl. warm-up/cool-down (~35 min). Keep B ≥24 h from heavy leg day, ideally >6 h from lifting |
| 3 (≈100–150) | 100–150 | Mon E 40 · Wed H (4×4) · Sat E 45–60 (long). Optional 10–15 min easy bike after lifting |
| 4 (≈150–210) | 150–210 | E 30–40 · H intervals 30–40 · tempo or E 30–45 · long E 60 (≈80/20) |
| 5 (≈200–300) | 200–300 | E 30–45 ×3 (one long 60–75) · H VO2max intervals · threshold/tempo or fartlek. Only if lifting volume is moderate; trim lifting volume ~10–20% if recovery lags |

Fat loss: same templates + daily step target; HIIT optional. Swap one E session for a 45–60 min ruck at ≤10–15% BM if desired.

## Decision rules

- IF beginner/sedentary THEN 3 × 20–30 min Z1 (talk test passes) → 150 min/wk over 6–8 wk → add intervals later. [1]
- IF goal = general health THEN 150–300 moderate-equivalent min/wk + strength ≥2 d/wk; count 1 vigorous min = 2 moderate. [1]
- IF time-poor (<3 h/wk) THEN 1–2 HIIT/SIT sessions (4×4 or 8×30/30) + 1 easy session. [5][8][9]
- IF no HR monitor THEN talk test / RPE. [12]
- IF user insists "must do Zone 2" THEN call it a good base and low-fatigue volume, not magic; keep ≥1 harder session if VO2max matters. [11]
- IF goal = endurance performance (recreational) THEN ~80% easy / ~20% hard, 1–2 hard sessions; IF trained and plateaued THEN polarized has a small edge, pyramidal acceptable. [3][4]
- IF goal = fat loss THEN pick the cardio they will keep doing; prioritise energy deficit and steps; HIIT ≈ MICT (small, inconsistent difference); IF choosing a single modality for fat mass THEN favour running/walking-type work over cycling-only. [9][10]
- IF goal = strength/hypertrophy THEN 2–3 low-impact sessions of 20–45 min, ≤1 interval session. [15]
- IF goal includes explosive power/sprinting THEN keep cardio ≥6 h from power/heavy-leg work or on separate days. [15]
- IF same-day lifting + cardio THEN lift first when strength/hypertrophy is the priority; cardio first (or ≥6 h apart) when cardio quality is the priority. Low-intensity cardio after lifting is fine. See [hybrid](10-hybrid.md).
- IF legs sore / heavy leg day THEN swap run for bike or upper-body-light cardio, or rest.
- IF planning more running volume THEN cap ~10%/wk as a soft target and never >30% in a week; IF novice with shin/knee pain THEN swap part of volume to cycling. [13]
- IF HR high at same pace, poor sleep, or flat mileage THEN deload week (−20–30%) and drop the hard session. `Practitioner`
- IF rucking THEN start ≤10% BM, 30 min; progress by ~5% BM or 10 min every 1–2 wk. [18]
- IF user has cardiac/metabolic disease or symptoms THEN clinician clearance first; no medical advice.

## Common mistakes

- Every session "moderately hard" (grey zone): too hard to recover from, too easy to drive VO2max.
- HIIT 4–6 d/wk, or on top of heavy leg days.
- Treating 220 − age as exact; ignoring talk test/RPE.
- Treating "Zone 2" as magic, or ignoring that its definition varies.
- Rigid 10% rule (or ignoring 30%+ jumps).
- Long running for leg-focused lifters; sprint intervals right before squats.
- HIIT as the fat-loss answer without addressing energy balance; cycling-only plan with fat-mass promises.
- No warm-up/cool-down; no progression or deload defined.
- Rucking too heavy too soon; calorie-calculator numbers treated as precise.

## Evidence notes

| Claim | Grade | Note |
|---|---|---|
| WHO/PAG dose | Strong | WHO exact wording read in full text; PAG 2nd ed. matches; 3rd edition in development |
| Older adults ≥3 d/wk multicomponent | Strong | WHO only; PAG gives no day count |
| Oliveira polarized edge | Moderate, narrow | GRADE high for VO2peak, but only <12 wk, highly trained; other endpoints null |
| HIIT ≥ MICT for VO2max | Moderate | Milanović healthy adults; Weston patients |
| HIIT vs MICT fat loss | Contested | Wewege (no diff; running yes, cycling no) vs Viana (+28.5% absolute fat mass for intervals) |
| Zone 2 not uniquely superior | Contested | Narrative review (Storoschuk 2025), abstract-verified; San-Millán lactate-based claims are practitioner opinion, not retrieved |
| Talk test = VT1 proxy | Moderate | Mostly cardiac patients |
| Helgerud 4×4 / 15/15 | Moderate | single RCT, n=40 moderately trained men |
| SIT | Moderate | not better than endurance training (d=0.04) |
| 10% rule | Moderate (no benefit) | Duration confound in GRONORUN |
| Rucking numbers | Moderate (secondary) | no controlled comparison with other cardio |
| Session/frequency tables, 10×1, example weeks | Practitioner | author synthesis |

Unverified and deliberately not stated as fact: Wilson 2012 duration/frequency thresholds (e.g. "50–60 vs 20–30 min/day").
