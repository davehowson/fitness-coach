# Session Design

> Purpose: build one complete workout (block order, warm-up, exercise count, sets/reps/RIR/rest, pairing, balance, cool-down) that fits a time budget.
> Use when: turning a split day or a one-off request into a concrete session; cutting a session to fit less time.
> Depends on: [variables](03-variables.md) (all numbers for sets, load, RIR, rest), [exercise library](12-exercise-library.md) (exercise choice), [splits](06-splits.md), [weekly planning](07-weekly-planning.md).

## Key rules (TL;DR)

1. **Block order:** warm-up → (optional power) → main compound → secondary compound → accessories/isolation → core → conditioning → optional cool-down. [1][2] Strong (strength), Moderate (hypertrophy)
2. **Warm-up = 5–10 min general + ramp sets of the first lift.** Never skip ramp sets, even in a 30-min session. Dynamic > static; static holds ≤60 s are harmless inside a full warm-up. [3][4] Moderate
3. **Exercises per session: 4–8 (45–75 min).** 3–5 at 30 min, 7–9 only at 90 min. Practitioner
4. **Per-muscle cap per session: 10 fractional hard sets (≈6–8 direct).** Beyond that, move volume to another day. Contested [see [variables](03-variables.md)]
5. **Use the numbers from [variables](03-variables.md):** default 2 RIR (3 RIR for beginners, older adults, heavy barbell compounds); rest 3 min heavy compound, 2 min hypertrophy compound, 90 s isolation; never default <60 s.
6. **Time-poor: antagonist supersets, not shorter compound rest.** They cut session time ~30–50% with no loss in hypertrophy or strength. Do not superset heavy (≥85% / ≤5 rep) main lifts. [5][6] Moderate
7. **Balance within a session:** include at least one push and one pull in a full-body or upper session; push:pull ≈ 1:1 or slightly more pulling. No trial supports a ratio for injury prevention. Practitioner
8. **Cool-down is optional.** It does not reduce soreness or speed recovery (low-quality evidence). Cut it first when short on time. [7][8] Moderate
9. **When cutting for time, cut in this order:** cool-down → conditioning finisher/extra core → 3rd+ isolation → sets 4+ of any exercise (keep ≥2 hard sets) → superset accessories → last, trim main lift to 2–3 sets. Keep one leg, one push, one pull multi-joint exercise. [6]

## 1. Block anatomy

| Block | Purpose | Reps | Sets | Rest | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Warm-up | Raise temperature, mobilise, activate, rehearse pattern | — | — | — | — |
| Power (optional: jumps, throws, Olympic derivatives) | Fast, low-fatigue; goes first because fatigue degrades velocity and technique | 1–5 | 3–5 | 1–3 min (full recovery) | Max velocity, stop before slowdown, 3+ RIR |
| Main lift | Heaviest, most technical pattern | 1–6 (strength); 5–8 (hypertrophy bias) | 3–5 (2–3 at 30 min) | 3 min (2–5) | 3 RIR for heavy barbell; strength 1–3 |
| Secondary compound | Second pattern or variation | 6–12 | 2–4 | 2 min (strength day 3) | 2 RIR |
| Accessory compound / machine | Volume with low systemic cost | 8–15 | 2–4 | 90 s–2 min | 0–2 RIR (default 2) |
| Isolation | Targets lagging or small muscles | 10–20 | 2–3 | 90 s (60–120) | last set 0–1 RIR |
| Core | Anti-extension, anti-rotation, lateral flexion | 8–15 or 20–45 s holds | 2–3 | 30–60 s | Moderate |
| Conditioning / finisher | Last so it does not impair lifting; see [conditioning](09-conditioning.md), [hybrid](10-hybrid.md) | per modality | 5–15 min | — | — |
| Cool-down | Optional | — | — | — | — |

Rest, RIR and rep values follow [variables](03-variables.md); the block-specific ranges above are Practitioner arrangements of those defaults. Why order matters: fatigue accumulates, so earlier exercises get more load and reps; strength gains are larger for exercises placed first (Nunes 2021), hypertrophy about equal. [2] Moderate

## 2. Warm-up protocol (RAMP)

RAMP (Jeffreys): **R**aise → **A**ctivate & **M**obilise → **P**otentiate. A practitioner framework, not a tested protocol as a whole. [9] Practitioner

| Phase | Dose | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Raise | 3–10 min easy bike/row/walk/dynamic movement until warm, light sweat | Practitioner [9][10] |
| Activate & mobilise | 4–6 dynamic drills, 1–2 sets of 5–10 reps, joints and muscles the session loads | Practitioner [10] |
| Potentiate (ramp sets, first lift) | See ladders below | Moderate–Practitioner [11][12] |
| Static stretch (only if wanted) | ≤30–45 s per muscle, ≤60 s max, only inside a full warm-up, followed by dynamic/specific work | Moderate [3][4] |
| Total | 5 min (30-min session) to 10–15 min (heavy session) | Practitioner |

Evidence: warm-ups improve performance in most studies (mostly non-lifting tasks, secondary figure ~80%). [13] Static stretching as the sole pre-effort warm-up slightly lowers strength/power (≈ −0.10 SD pooled; smallest with holds ≤45 s). [3] Specific sets near working load can improve maximal strength and reps, evidence mixed. [11][12] Number, load and rest of ramp sets are not established.

### Ramp-set ladders (percent of the first lift's top working weight)

Ramp sets are not working volume; do not count them. Rest 30–90 s between ramp sets (Practitioner).

| Starting lift | Ladder | Sets |
|---|---|---|
| Heavy compound (≥85% 1RM or ≤5 reps), strength | 40–50% × 5 → 60–70% × 3–5 → 80–85% × 1–3 → (optional) 90% × 1 | 3–4 (up to 5 for a 90-min top-set day) |
| Moderate compound (hypertrophy, 6–12 reps) | 50% × 8 → 70% × 4–5 → 85% × 2 | 2–3 |
| Accessory-only or light session | 50% × 8 of the first exercise | 1–2 |
| Time ≤30 min | 50% × 5 → 75% × 3 | 2 |
| Power first | 2–3 low-amplitude jump/throw sets, then block; no static stretching first | 2–3 |

Example: top set 100 kg × 5 → 45 kg × 5, 65 kg × 4, 82.5 kg × 2 (then 90 kg × 1 only if the top set is ≥90% 1RM). Round to available plates. Later lifts in the session need only 1 light set (50–60% × 5–8) if the pattern is new or joints are stiff; otherwise start at working weight.

## 3. How many exercises and sets

| Quantity | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Exercises per session | **4–8** (45–75 min); fewer with heavy lifts | Practitioner |
| Total working sets | 12–25 | Scales with time (Practitioner) |
| Hard sets per muscle per session | cap **10 fractional** (≈6–8 direct) | Contested |
| Sets per exercise | 2–4; strength 2–3; peaking up to 5 | Moderate |
| Beginner | 3–5 exercises, 2–3 sets, full body, 6–15 reps | Practitioner [6] |
| Minimal dose (2 × 30 min/wk) | 1 knee-dominant (squat/leg press), 1 push, 1 pull per session; ≥4 sets/muscle/wk at 6–15 reps | Moderate [6] |

Fractional check: when summing a session, bench adds 1.0 to chest and 0.5 to triceps and front delts, rows add 0.5 to biceps and rear delts, and so on ([variables §1.1](03-variables.md)). Stop adding sets for a muscle once it is at the cap in this session.

## 4. Time budgets

Assumption: ~2.5–4.5 min per straight compound set including rest and transition, ~1.5–2.5 min per isolation set. Supersets cut this by ~30–50%. Practitioner arithmetic, not research-derived. [5][6]

| Session | Warm-up | Main block | Accessory | Core / conditioning | Working sets | Exercises |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30 min | 4–5 | 1 compound × 3 sets (~12 min) | 2–3 exercises supersetted (~10) | 2–3 | 10–14 | 3–5 |
| 45 min | 6–7 | 1–2 compounds × 3 (~18) | 2–3 supersetted (~15) | ~4 | 14–18 | 4–6 |
| 60 min | 8–10 | main 3–4 + secondary 3 (~25) | 3 exercises (~18) | ~5 | 18–22 | 5–7 |
| 75 min | ~10 | main 4–5 + secondary 3–4 (~30) | 4 exercises (~20) | ~10 | 22–28 | 6–8 |
| 90 min | 10–12 | main 5 + secondary 4 (~35) | 4–5 exercises (~25) | 10–12 | 26–34 | 7–9 |

Do not exceed 10 fractional sets per muscle at any length; use the extra time for longer rest on the main lift (3–5 min on heavy sets), not more sets.

### What to cut first, in order

1. Cool-down and stretching. [7][8]
2. Conditioning finisher and extra core.
3. Third-plus isolation exercise and redundant accessories.
4. Sets 4+ of any exercise (never go below 2 hard sets per kept exercise).
5. Superset antagonist or non-competing accessories instead of lengthening the session. [5][6]
6. Last resort: main lift to 2–3 sets and ramp to 2 sets. Keep the main compound plus one leg, one push, one pull multi-joint exercise. [6]

## 5. Supersets and pairing

| Type | Example | When | Time saving | Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Antagonist | Bench + row; curl + pushdown | Default time-saver for accessories and moderate compounds | ~30–50% | Higher perceived exertion |
| Non-competing (upper/lower) | Press + split squat | Compounds with low interference | ~30–40% | Cardiovascular load |
| Agonist (same muscle) | Incline press + fly | Hypertrophy finisher, isolation only | Moderate | Volume load falls (SMD −1.08); cut load 5–10% |
| Tri-set / giant set | 3–4 exercises, 1 region | Pump finisher, short sessions | Large | Fatigue, quality drops |
| Circuit | 5–8 stations | General fitness, ≤30 min | Large | Weak for max strength |

Evidence: 19-study meta-analysis (n=313): agonist-antagonist pairs raise reps completed (SMD +0.68), same-biomechanics pairs cut volume load (SMD −1.08), hypertrophy no difference (SMD −0.05), sessions shorter without strength or hypertrophy loss, higher perceived exertion and muscle damage. [5] Moderate (short-term studies). Time-saving percentages are Practitioner arithmetic from [5][6].

Pairing rules:
- IF time-constrained THEN antagonist or non-competing supersets for accessories first, compounds second. Rest 60–90 s after each pair; compound rest stays ≥90 s.
- IF the lift is a heavy main lift (≤5 reps or ≥85%) THEN no superset; full rest.
- IF goal is maximal strength THEN supersets on accessories only.
- IF form degrades or user is gassed THEN revert to straight sets.
- IF paired exercises share a muscle THEN isolation only or drop load 5–10%.
- IF time is not a constraint THEN straight sets (best supported, easiest to progress).

## 6. Pattern balance within a session and across the week

- Full-body or upper session: ≥1 push and ≥1 pull. Lower session: ≥1 knee-dominant, ≥1 hinge. Push:pull ≈ 1:1 (or slightly more pulling). Practitioner [14]
- Weekly pattern targets (Practitioner defaults, general trainee):

| Pattern | Weekly hard sets |
|---|---|
| Horizontal push : horizontal pull | 1 : 1 to 1 : 1.5 |
| Vertical push : vertical pull | 1 : 1 to 1 : 2 |
| Knee-dominant : hip-dominant | 1 : 1 (pick lower-fatigue hinges such as RDL or hip thrust when fatigue is high) |
| Unilateral share of lower-body work | 20–40% |
| Core / anti-rotation | 2–3 exercises, 6–10 sets |
| Carries | 1–2 per week, 2–4 sets of 20–40 m or 30–60 s |

- IF pressing volume exceeds ~1.5× pulling THEN add pulling sets.
- IF the week has no hinge or no single-leg work THEN add one hinge and one unilateral lower exercise.
- IF user has a side-to-side asymmetry or wants single-leg strength THEN add unilateral work. IF user needs max bilateral strength THEN prioritise bilateral lifts. Unilateral vs bilateral: equal hypertrophy; strength follows specificity. [15] Moderate-low
- Weekly distribution across days: [splits](06-splits.md), [weekly planning](07-weekly-planning.md). Exercise choice per pattern: [exercise library](12-exercise-library.md).

## 7. Ordering rules inside the session

- IF strength goal THEN power → main lift → secondary → accessories.
- IF hypertrophy and a muscle is lagging THEN its exercise first.
- IF two exercises are equally important THEN alternate which goes first week to week.
- IF an exercise is technical (Olympic lift, heavy squat) THEN before fatiguing work.
- IF grip-limited pull (deadlift) THEN before heavy rows/pulls.
- IF a core exercise taxes bracing heavily (hard ab wheel) THEN after main lifts.
- IF conditioning is included THEN after lifting unless endurance is the day's priority. Same-session lift-first if strength is the priority; see [hybrid](10-hybrid.md).

## 8. Cool-down

- Optional. 3–5 min easy walk or bike plus optional light stretching, if the user likes it. [7][8]
- Cochrane review: stretching before/after exercise gives no clinically important soreness reduction (~1 point on 100-point scale at 24 h). Meta-analysis of 10 RCTs (n=229): no effect of post-exercise stretching on DOMS at 24/48/72 h or strength recovery; very low confidence. No evidence of harm. Moderate for "no clear benefit"
- IF user asks whether stretching reduces soreness THEN say evidence shows no clinically important effect. Psychological relaxation and ROM benefits are untested.

## 9. Worked session templates

Notation: sets × reps @ RIR, rest. SS = superset (alternate exercises, rest after each pair). RIR defaults per [variables](03-variables.md): 2 RIR, 3 RIR for heavy barbell compounds and beginners, isolation last set 0–1. Replace exercise categories with specific lifts from the [exercise library](12-exercise-library.md). Set sums are fractional hard sets.

### T1. 45-min full body (beginner/intermediate, 3×/wk)

| Block | Exercise category | Prescription |
|---|---|---|
| Warm-up (6 min) | 3 min bike/row + 4 dynamic drills + 2 ramp sets (50% × 8, 75% × 3) of squat/leg press | — |
| A | Squat or leg press | 3 × 6–10 @3 (beginner) or @2 (machine), 2 min |
| B1/B2 SS | Horizontal press / horizontal row | 3 × 8–12 @2, 90 s after pair |
| C1/C2 SS | Hip hinge (RDL) / vertical pull (pulldown) | 2–3 × 8–12 @2, 90 s |
| D1/D2 SS | Lateral raise / core anti-extension | 2 × 12–15 @1 / 2 × 30 s, 60–90 s |
| Cool-down | Optional 3 min walk | — |

Check: 10–11 working sets, 5 exercise slots + core; quads 3, chest 3, back 5 (row + pulldown) etc. well under cap.

### T2. 30-min minimal-dose full body (2×/wk)

| Block | Prescription |
|---|---|
| Warm-up 4 min | 2 min easy cardio + 2 ramp sets of first lift |
| SS1: squat/leg press + row | 3 × 8–12 @2, 90 s |
| SS2: press (push-up/bench) + pulldown/pull-up | 3 × 8–12 @2, 90 s |
| Finisher: RDL or hip thrust | 2 × 10–12 @2, 90 s |

Check: ≥4 weekly sets per muscle at 6–15 reps over two sessions (squat 6, press 6, pull 12 incl. row, hinge 4). [6]

### T3. 60-min upper (hypertrophy, upper/lower 4×/wk)

| Block | Prescription |
|---|---|
| Warm-up 8 min | 3 min raise, band/arm circles, 3 ramp sets bench (50% × 8, 70% × 4, 85% × 2) |
| A. Horizontal press (main) | 4 × 5–8 @3, 3 min |
| B. Horizontal row | 4 × 6–10 @2, 2 min |
| C1/C2 SS | Incline DB press / chest-supported vertical pull: 3 × 8–12 @1–2, 90 s |
| D1/D2 SS | Lateral raise / rear-delt fly: 3 × 12–20 @0–1 (last set), 60–90 s |
| E1/E2 SS | Biceps curl / triceps extension: 3 × 10–15 @0–1 (last set), 60–90 s |
| Core | Pallof press 2 × 10 per side, 60 s |

Check: chest 7 direct + 0 indirect other = 7 (under cap); triceps 3 direct + 3.5 indirect = 6.5; lats/back ~7 direct. Weekly total ≈ 14 chest sets over two upper days: advanced-range volume; for intermediate (12) drop C to 2 sets.

### T4. 75-min lower (strength + hypertrophy)

| Block | Prescription |
|---|---|
| Warm-up 10 min | 4 min raise, hip/ankle mobility, 4 ramp sets squat (45% × 5, 65% × 4, 82% × 2, 90% × 1) |
| Power (optional) | Box jumps / med-ball throws 3 × 3, 1–2 min |
| A. Back squat (main) | 4 × 4–6 @2–3, 3–4 min |
| B. RDL / hinge | 3 × 6–10 @3, 2–3 min |
| C. Split squat / lunge | 3 × 8–12 per leg @2, 90 s–2 min |
| D1/D2 SS | Leg curl / calf raise: 3 × 10–15 @1 (last set 0), 60–90 s |
| E. Core | Loaded carry 3 × 30–40 m + ab wheel 2 × 8–12 |
| Conditioning (optional) | 5–10 min easy bike |

Check: quads 4 direct squat + 3 split squat = 7 direct (+ glute indirect 3.5); hamstrings ~6 (RDL 3 + curl 3). Under 10 fractional per muscle.

### T5. 90-min strength day (advanced, 3–4 d/wk)

| Block | Prescription |
|---|---|
| Warm-up 12 min | Full RAMP; 5 ramp sets to top set (40% × 5, 60% × 3, 75% × 3, 85% × 1–2, 90% × 1) |
| A. Main lift (e.g. squat) | 5 × 3–5 @2–3, 4–5 min |
| B. Secondary (pause squat / front squat) | 3 × 5–8 @2, 3 min |
| C1/C2 SS | Press/pull complement (e.g. overhead press / chin-up): 4 × 6–10 @2, 2 min |
| D. Accessories (3 exercises, SS pairs) | 3 × 8–15 @1–2, 90 s |
| E. Core + carries | 3 sets, 60 s |

Check: quads direct 8 sets on this day (A 5 + B 3) plus indirect; do not add more lower-body accessories; main-lift weekly sets stay near 6 per lift per the strength default if A is split across 2 days, so use this template on 2 of 4 days with different lifts. Strength benefits less from supersets, so only C and D are paired. [5]

### T6. 60-min full body, dumbbells only (general fitness, 3×/wk)

| Block | Prescription |
|---|---|
| Warm-up 7 min | 3 min raise, 4 drills, 2 ramp sets goblet squat (light DB × 8, working DB × 3) |
| A. Goblet / DB front squat | 3 × 8–12 @2, 2 min |
| B1/B2 SS | DB bench or floor press / one-arm DB row: 3 × 8–12 @2, 90 s |
| C1/C2 SS | DB RDL / DB overhead press: 3 × 8–12 @2, 90 s |
| D1/D2 SS | Reverse lunge or split squat (per leg) / lateral raise: 2 × 10–15 @1–2, 60–90 s |
| E | Carry (suitcase) 3 × 30–40 m + plank 2 × 30–45 s | 
| Cool-down | Optional 3–5 min walk |

Check: 17–18 working sets in ~60 min; every major pattern ≥1 hard set; push:pull 1:1. If DBs cap load (≤25 kg) THEN go to 15–25 reps at 0–2 RIR ([variables §2.2](03-variables.md)).

### T7. 45-min upper/lower hybrid with conditioning finisher (strength priority, lift-then-cardio)

| Block | Prescription |
|---|---|
| Warm-up 6 min | 3 min raise, 3 ramp sets of deadlift (50% × 5, 70% × 3, 85% × 1–2) |
| A. Deadlift or trap-bar | 3 × 3–5 @3, 3 min |
| B1/B2 | Pull-up or pulldown / push-up or DB press: 3 × 6–10 @2, 2 min |
| C. Single-leg (split squat) | 2 × 8–10 per leg @2, 90 s |
| Conditioning (8 min) | Easy-to-moderate bike or incline walk, talk-test pace |

Rationale: lifting first protects strength when strength is the priority; hard intervals stay away from heavy leg days. See [hybrid](10-hybrid.md), [conditioning](09-conditioning.md).

## Decision rules

- IF session starts with a heavy compound (≥85% 1RM or ≤5 reps) THEN 5 min general + 3–4 ramp sets rising to ~85–90% of the top set, fewer reps as load rises.
- IF accessory-only or light session THEN 3–5 min general + 1–2 light sets of the first exercise.
- IF user wants stretching before training THEN dynamic work, or static holds ≤30–45 s followed by dynamic/specific work.
- IF time ≤30 min THEN keep warm-up ~5 min, never skip ramp sets, 3–5 exercises, antagonist supersets, 10–14 sets.
- IF time = 45 THEN 4–6 exercises, 1 main + supersetted accessories. IF 60 THEN 5–7 exercises, 18–22 sets. IF 75–90 THEN 6–9 exercises, 22–34 sets, ≤10 fractional sets/muscle, lengthen main-lift rest to 3–5 min.
- IF a muscle already has ≥6–8 direct (≈10 fractional) hard sets in this session THEN stop adding sets for it.
- IF weekly target for a muscle >~10–12 sets THEN split across ≥2 sessions.
- IF beginner THEN 3–5 exercises, 2–3 sets each, full body, 6–15 reps, 3 RIR, 90–120 s rest.
- IF the plan has no hinge, single-leg or core work in the week THEN add them.
- IF planning rest intervals THEN use [variables §5](03-variables.md); do not re-derive.

## Common mistakes

- Static stretching 60+ s as the only warm-up before heavy or explosive work. [3]
- No ramp sets, or so many near-max warm-up singles that they fatigue the top set.
- Isolation or core before the main compound lift.
- >10 fractional sets for one muscle in a single session, assuming a once-a-week "destroy it" session is necessary.
- 10+ exercises in 45 min with 30 s rests, collapsing load and reps (rest <60 s lowers volume load).
- Supersetting heavy compounds that share a muscle (volume load drops).
- Push-heavy plans (bench/OHP/dips/flyes) with token pulling.
- No hinge, no single-leg, no carries/core across the week.
- Ignoring indirect volume (bench trains triceps) when estimating per-session sets.
- Prescribing cool-down stretching as necessary for recovery.
- Time budgets that ignore rest and transition time.
- Counting ramp sets as working volume.

## Evidence notes

- **Order:** strength gains larger for exercises placed first (11 studies, ≤12 wk); hypertrophy order-independent in short trials (g≈0.03). Long-term effects unknown. [2] Moderate
- **Warm-up:** Fradkin ~80% figure is a secondary coaching-summary number. [13] Specific-sets evidence: a systematic review of 11 studies found no consensus; a 40-man study found 6 sets at 80% of working load gave higher squat mean propulsive velocity than 40%. [11][12] RAMP as a whole untested. Moderate-low
- **Static stretching:** older meta-analysis says avoid as sole warm-up; Behm 2016 says fine (≤60 s) inside a full warm-up. [3][4]
- **Per-session cap:** ~10 fractional soft cap rests on a preprint point (~11 fractional); the "6–10 direct sets per session" heuristic has no primary source. Contested
- **Supersets:** short-term studies; advanced-lifter effects unclear; minimal-effective-dose guidance may not apply to athletes. [5][6] Moderate
- **Time budgets, exercise counts, push:pull ratio, knee:hip ratio, carries, anti-rotation:** Practitioner; no trial-tested ratios. Occupational push/pull research does not translate to gym ratios. [14]
- **Rest:** ACSM 2026 gives no inter-set rest guidance; the 3–5 min heavy-lift figure is ACSM 2009. [16] See [variables](03-variables.md).
- **Unilateral vs bilateral:** 2025 meta-analysis (9 studies), wide CIs. [15] Moderate-low
