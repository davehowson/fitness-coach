# Workout Design Wiki

A reference for LLMs that design workout plans. Everything needed to write an evidence-based plan is here, so **no research is needed at generation time**. Numbers come from meta-analyses, position stands (ACSM 2026, WHO 2020) and graded practitioner consensus, cross-checked in a verification pass.

## How to use this wiki

1. Follow **[00-workflow.md](00-workflow.md)** step by step. It is the procedure; the other pages are the reference it points to.
2. Use the numbers on these pages instead of recalling numbers from memory. Where pages disagree with general knowledge, these pages win.
3. Respect evidence grades. Rules marked `Strong` are firm defaults. `Moderate` rules are good defaults. `Practitioner` rules are coaching convention that can be adjusted to preference. `Contested` rules are options, not requirements.
4. Before returning any plan, run **[14-quality-checklist.md](14-quality-checklist.md)**.

## The core idea

A good workout is a **fit between a plan and a person**. It serves the goal (specificity), gives enough hard work to drive adaptation (overload), stays within what the person can recover from, progresses by a stated rule, fits real constraints (days, time, equipment, experience, injuries), and is something the person will actually do. When two options are close, **adherence decides**.

For most people, the **main lever is weekly hard sets per muscle**. Frequency, split choice and periodization model matter much less than total volume, effort and consistent progression.

## Quick defaults (details in linked pages)

| Item | Default |
|---|---|
| Frequency | Each muscle 2×/week |
| Weekly hard sets/muscle (hypertrophy) | Beginner 8 · Intermediate 12 (10–20) · Advanced 16 |
| Weekly hard sets (strength, main lifts) | ~6 (2–3 sets × 2–3 sessions), heavy |
| Per-session soft cap | 10 fractional sets per muscle |
| Effort | 2 RIR; beginners/older adults/heavy barbell 3 RIR |
| Rest | 3 min heavy compound · 2 min hypertrophy compound · 90 s isolation |
| Progression | Double progression; linear per session for beginner barbell lifts |
| Deload | ~1 week, −30–50% volume, every 4–8 hard weeks or when fatigued |
| Cardio floor | 150–300 min moderate or 75–150 min vigorous per week |
| Strength + cardio | Lift first if strength is the priority; ≥6 h apart or separate days |

## Scope and limits

- Healthy adults are the default. Special populations are covered in [11-populations.md](11-populations.md) at a general level.
- **No medical advice.** Refer to a professional for pain, injury, pregnancy complications, cardiovascular symptoms or any red flag in [02-intake.md](02-intake.md).
- Nutrition is covered only where it changes program design (e.g. training in a calorie deficit).
