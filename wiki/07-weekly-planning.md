# Weekly Planning: Days-per-Week Playbook

> Purpose: given days available per week and a goal, pick a split, compute sets per muscle per session, and place sessions (and cardio) on a Mon–Sun calendar.
> Use when: building or adjusting a weekly schedule; the user states how many days they can train; combining splits; checking recovery spacing.
> Depends on: [splits](06-splits.md) (layout detail per split), [principles](01-principles.md), [variables](03-variables.md), [goals](04-goals.md), [session design](05-session-design.md), [conditioning](09-conditioning.md), [hybrid](10-hybrid.md)

## Key rules (TL;DR)

1. **Weekly hard sets per muscle drive results; days/week only sets how those sets are distributed.** At equal volume, frequency has little independent effect on hypertrophy [2] (Strong) or strength [3] (Moderate).
2. **Default 2×/muscle/week.** 1× only at <~10 weekly sets or when 1 day is all that exists; 3× to keep per-session sets down at high volume.
3. **Arithmetic:** sets per muscle per session = weekly target ÷ frequency. Canonical weekly targets: hypertrophy beginner **8**, intermediate **12**, advanced/priority **16**; max strength **6** (2–3 sets × 2–3 sessions); maintenance **4–6**; minimum useful ≥1 hard set per major pattern on ≥2 d/wk. Fractional counting: direct 1, indirect 0.5.
4. **Per-session soft cap: 10 fractional hard sets per muscle (≈6–8 direct).** If target ÷ frequency exceeds it, add a session or lower the target. Session total: 4–8 exercises, 45–75 min.
5. **Default map:** 1 d FB · 2 d FB×2 · 3 d FB×3 · 4 d UL×2 · 5 d ULPPL · 6 d PPL×2 · 7 d = max 5–6 lifting days + easy/active days. Never 7 hard days.
6. **Recovery spacing:** ≥48 h between hard sessions of the same muscle (72 h preferred for large muscles at high volume); ≤3 consecutive hard days; no heavy squat → heavy deadlift/heavy leg day on consecutive days.
7. **Cardio:** health floor 150–300 min/wk moderate or 75–150 min/wk vigorous + muscle strengthening of all major groups ≥2 d/wk. Lifters' cardio 2–3 sessions/wk is safe; prefer cycling/rowing/incline walking; keep hard intervals away from heavy leg days; same-day: lift first if strength is the priority, ≥6 h apart or separate days (Schumann 2022: negligible effect on max strength/hypertrophy; explosive strength only, if <3 h) [11].
8. Goal changes **volume and cardio**, not the base mapping: strength favors lift-specific frequency (2–3×/lift); hypertrophy favors 2×/muscle; fat loss keeps lifting and cuts sets before frequency.
9. Fewer than the ideal days is fine: choose the split that still gives 2×/muscle before choosing a fancier one. Detailed hybrid weeks: [hybrid](10-hybrid.md).

## Core concepts

- **Training days ≠ muscle frequency.** Frequency = sessions per week giving a muscle meaningful direct sets (≥~3 hard sets) or meaningful indirect work.
- **Frequency as a volume-distribution tool.** More sessions → fewer sets per session → higher-quality sets and less junk volume [4]. Not a growth lever by itself (Strong).
- **Recovery window:** ≈48–72 h to retrain a muscle (Practitioner). 2×/wk ≈ 3–4 days apart (Mon/Thu, Tue/Fri); 3×/wk ≈ every other day.
- **Volume dose-response:** each added weekly set ≈ +0.37% muscle gain up to ≥10 sets/wk, diminishing returns ~18–20 [5] (Moderate–Strong; little data above ~20).
- **Hard/easy structure:** with 5+ days not every day can be hard. Plan heavy/high-volume days, then easy (light/technique/Zone 2) days.
- **Legend used below:** U upper · L lower · FB full body · Pu/Pl/Lg push/pull/legs · C cardio · Z2 easy aerobic · INT intervals/tempo · R rest · AR active recovery (walk/mobility).

## Frequency achieved and sets-per-session arithmetic

Sets per session = weekly target ÷ frequency. "Exceeds cap" = >8 direct or >10 fractional per muscle per session.

| Days | Split | Freq | Str 6 | Maint 4–6 | Beg 8 | Int 12 | Adv 16 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FB | 1× | 6 | 4–6 | 8 (edge of cap; 75 min) | 12 exceeds cap | 16 exceeds cap |
| 2 | FB×2 | 2× | 3 | 2–3 | 4 | 6 | 8 (edge; FB session too long, see below) |
| 3 | FB×3 | 3× | 2 | 1.3–2 (use 2) | ≈2.7 → 3/3/2 | 4 | ≈5.3 → 5/5/6 |
| 4 | UL×2 | 2× | 3 | 2–3 | 4 | 6 | 8 |
| 5 | ULPPL | 2× (per pattern) | 3 | 2–3 | 4 | 6 | 8 |
| 6 | PPL×2 | 2× | 3 | 2–3 | 4 | 6 | 8 |
| 6 | U/L×3 | 3× | 2 | 1.3–2 | ≈2.7 | 4 | ≈5.3 |
| 7 | 5–6 lifting d | 2–3× | as 5–6 d | | | | |

Rules from the arithmetic:
- **FB total-set ceiling.** An FB session covers ~6–8 muscle groups. At 4–8 exercises × 2–4 sets (≈12–24 hard sets), compound crossover (0.5 credit) lets each muscle get ~3–6 sets. So FB reaches 12/wk at 3 d (4 per session) but **16/wk needs ≥4 d or a split**.
- **1 day cannot reach intermediate volume.** Realistic output: 3–6 sets per muscle = maintenance to modest growth; ≥8 requires ~2 exercises per pattern (≈75 min).
- Indirect credit: 3 sets of bench = 3 chest + 1.5 triceps + 1.5 front delt. Subtract before adding direct triceps/delt sets.
- Example (U/L×2, intermediate 12/wk triceps): 2 pressing sessions × 6 chest-pressing sets = 6 indirect (6 × 0.5 × 2 sessions = 6 fractional) → add ~6 direct triceps sets/wk (3 per upper day) to reach 12.

## Days-per-week playbook

### 1 day
- **Ranked options:** (1) Full body (6–8 exercises: squat or leg press, hinge, horizontal push, horizontal pull, vertical push or pull, core, carry), 2–3 sets each, 70–85% 1RM, 1–3 RIR. Only one rational option.
- **Frequency:** 1× per muscle. **Arithmetic:** target 8 → 8 per session (edge of cap) is only achievable with ≈75 min and 2 exercises per pattern; otherwise expect 3–6 sets/muscle. Intermediate 12 and advanced 16 are unreachable; be explicit that this is a maintenance-to-modest-gain dose. Strength still improves (suboptimal) [6][7]. Add daily walking.
- **Calendars:**

| Option | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A (strength/general) | R | R | R | R | R | FB (hard) | R |
| B (+ walking) | R | Walk | R | Walk | R | FB | Walk |

- **By goal:** strength → pick the 2–3 target lifts (squat/bench/deadlift or press/pull/hinge), 2–3 sets each at ≥80% 1RM, 1–6 reps, 3 min rest. Hypertrophy → 8 sets/muscle max, RIR 2. General fitness → 1 hard set per major pattern minimum is met; add walks. Fat loss → same FB plus daily steps. Hybrid → 1 lift + 2–3 cardio days (see [hybrid](10-hybrid.md)); lifting on the day after rest.
- **Cardio:** easy Z2 / walks on non-lift days; no hard intervals on the day before the lift day.

### 2 days
- **Ranked options:** (1) FB A/B, 3+ days apart (2×/muscle); (2) U/L (1× each; only if weekly sets <10/muscle); (3) hybrid FB + FB + Z2.
- **Frequency:** FB 2×, U/L 1×. **Arithmetic (FB):** Beginner 8 → 4/session; intermediate 12 → 6/session; advanced 16 → 8 (too long; cap at 12). Strength 6 → 3 per lift-muscle per session. Meets the ≥2 d/wk strengthening guideline [12].
- **Calendars:**

| Option | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: FB/FB | FB-A | R | R | FB-B | R | R/walk | R |
| B: FB/FB (weekend) | R | R | FB-A | R | R | FB-B | Walk |
| C: U/L (low volume) | U | R | R | L | R | Z2 | R |
| D: hybrid | FB | R | Z2 | R | FB | R | Long Z2 |

- **By goal:** strength → FB with main lift rotation (A: squat/bench, B: hinge/press), 3–5 sets on the focus lift, ≥80% 1RM. Hypertrophy → 12 sets/muscle at 6/session; accept a lower ceiling. General fitness → FB×2 + Z2 ≥150 min/wk spread over off days. Fat loss → FB×2 + steps; keep intensity. Hybrid → add 1–2 Z2 days (Option D).
- **Cardio:** Wed/Sat Z2 or long Z2 Sunday. Keep intervals ≥24 h from FB days.

### 3 days
- **Ranked options:** (1) FB A/B/A (alternate weeks B/A/B); (2) U/L/FB (intermediate, hypertrophy); (3) PPL once (1×; lowest rank, preference only).
- **Frequency:** FB 3×; U/L/FB ≈2× (FB gives the third exposure to each); PPL 1×. **Arithmetic (FB×3):** Beginner 8 → ≈3/session; intermediate 12 → 4/session; advanced 16 → ≈5/session (hard to fit; cap ≈12–14 on 3 d, or go to 4 d). Strength 6 → 2/session per lift-muscle.
- **Calendars:**

| Option | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: FB A/B/A | FB-A | R | FB-B | R | FB-A | R | R |
| B: U/L/FB | U | R | L | R | FB | Z2 | R |
| C: FB + cardio | FB | Z2 | FB | R | FB | Z2 (long) | R |
| D: PPL once | Pu | R | Pl | R | Lg | R | R |

- **By goal:** strength → FB×3 with heavy / light / medium days of the main lift (2–3×/lift). Hypertrophy → U/L/FB or FB with exercise variation per day. General fitness → FB×3 + 2 cardio days. Fat loss → FB×3 + Z2/steps on off days; keep protein/loading. Hybrid → 3 lift + 2 cardio (Option C); see [hybrid](10-hybrid.md).
- **Cardio:** 2 non-lifting days Z2; optional ≤10 min finisher after lifting. Do not place intervals the day before FB with squats/deadlifts.

### 4 days
- **Ranked options:** (1) Upper/Lower ×2; (2) PHUL (power/hypertrophy U/L); (3) Torso/Limbs (arm/leg emphasis); (4) FB×4 low volume (sessions <40 min); (5) Anterior/Posterior.
- **Frequency:** 2×. **Arithmetic:** Beginner 8 → 4/session; intermediate 12 → 6/session; advanced 16 → 8/session (crowded upper day: consider TL or 5 d). Strength 6 → 3/session per lift-muscle. FB×4: 12/wk → 3/session.
- **Calendars:**

| Option | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: U/L | U | L | R | U | L | R/C | R |
| B: U/L (spread legs) | U | R | L | R | U | L | R |
| C: PHUL | U-power | L-power | R | U-hyp | L-hyp | C | R |
| D: 4 lift + 1–2 C | U | L | Z2 | U | L | Long Z2 | R |
| E: FB×4 low volume | FB | R | FB | R | FB | FB (light) | R |

- **By goal:** strength → U/L with lift heavy on one day, moderate/volume on the other (2–3×/lift), or PHUL. Hypertrophy → U/L or TL, 6 sets/muscle/session at 12/wk. General fitness → U/L + 1–2 cardio. Fat loss → U/L + steps/Z2 on off days. Hybrid → Options D; hard INT on Saturday only.
- **Cardio:** Z2 on 1–2 off days, long Z2 on the weekend; INT ≤1/wk and not the day before a lower day.

### 5 days
- **Ranked options:** (1) ULPPL (U, L, Pu, Pl, Lg); (2) U/L ×2 + 1 specialization/weak-point or cardio day; (3) PHAT (advanced); (4) UL + FB; (5) bro split (1×, lowest rank).
- **Frequency:** 2× per pattern for ULPPL (push muscles: U + Pu; pull: U + Pl; legs: L + Lg). **Arithmetic:** Beginner (not recommended) 8 → 4; intermediate 12 → 6/session; advanced 16 → 8. Delts/triceps/biceps receive extra indirect work on both U and Pu/Pl: subtract fractionally (e.g. triceps intermediate 12 = ~6 indirect + ~6 direct split over 2 sessions). Specialization: priority 16 at 3× → ≈5/session; others 4–6/wk.
- **Calendars:**

| Option | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: ULPPL | U | L | R | Pu | Pl | Lg | R |
| B: PPL + UL | Pu | Pl | Lg | R | U | L | R |
| C: UL + specialization | U | L | R | U | L | Weak-point / C | R |
| D: UL + FB | U | L | R | U | L | FB (light) | R |

- Note A: legs Tue and Sat (4 days apart); Pl→Lg adjacent Fri→Sat: keep Friday pull light on hinge/low-back loading. B: Lg Wed and L Sat (3 days).
- **By goal:** strength → U/L ×2 + a third lower/lift-specific day; keep ≤2 heavy leg days. Hypertrophy → ULPPL. General fitness → not needed; use 3–4 d + cardio. Fat loss → cap at 4 lifting + steps; if 5, cut sets 20–30%. Hybrid → 3 lift + 2 cardio.
- **Cardio:** needs one clear rest or cardio day. Place C on Wed/Sun (Z2). INT only on a day away from legs.

### 6 days
- **Ranked options:** (1) PPL×2; (2) ULPPL+ (U, L, Pu, Pl, Lg, Arms/delts); (3) U/L ×3 (3× per muscle, 4/session at 12); (4) 4 lift + 2 cardio (hybrid); (5) Arnold (advanced only).
- **Frequency:** PPL×2 2×; U/L×3 3×. **Arithmetic:** PPL×2: intermediate 12 → 6/session; advanced 16 → 8/session; beginner not recommended. U/L×3: 12 → 4; 16 → ≈5.3.
- **Calendars:**

| Option | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: PPL×2 | Pu | Pl | Lg | Pu | Pl | Lg | R |
| B: ULPPL+ | U | L | Pu | Pl | Lg | Arms/delts (+C) | R |
| C: U/L×3 | U | L | R | U | L | U/FB (light) | R |
| D: 4 lift + 2 C | U | L | Z2 | U | L | Long C | R |

- Note A: legs Wed and Sat (3 days). Vary day A vs day B (heavy vs volume). B: Lg Fri then arms/delts Sat is fine; L Tue and Lg Fri leave 3 days.
- **By goal:** hypertrophy → PPL×2; strength → not a primary use (4–5 d with 2–3×/lift suffices); general fitness / fat loss → not recommended, offer 4–5 d + cardio; hybrid → Option D.
- **Gate:** IF not advanced OR sleeps <7 h THEN reduce to 5 d.
- **Cardio:** Z2 on Wed (Option D) or short post-upper finisher. Skip INT or limit to 1.

### 7 days
- **Rule:** maximum 5–6 resistance days; ≥1 day genuinely easy (walk/mobility/Z2) and ideally 1 full rest day. **Never 7 hard days.**
- **Ranked options:** (1) 5 lift + 2 cardio/mobility; (2) PPL×2 + 1 AR day; (3) daily light FB (low-ranked: ≤3–5 sets/pattern/day, ≥2 easy days; no evidence of benefit over 4–5 d at equal volume [2][3]).
- **Frequency:** 2–3×. **Arithmetic:** same as the 5/6-day split chosen.
- **Calendars:**

| Option | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: 5 lift + 2 C | U | L | Z2 | U | L | FB (light) | Long Z2 / AR |
| B: PPL×2 + AR | Pu | Pl | Lg | Pu | Pl | Lg | AR |
| C: daily light FB | FB-H | FB-E | FB-E | FB-H | FB-E | FB-E | AR |

- **By goal:** hypertrophy → Option B (PPL×2) only with advanced recovery; general fitness / fat loss → Option A; hybrid → [hybrid](10-hybrid.md) 7-day tables (one day easy).
- IF recovery signals worsen (performance falls 2 sessions in a row) THEN drop to 5 d.

## How the schedule changes by goal

| Goal | Preferred days | Preferred splits | Volume target | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strength | 3–5 | FB×3, UL×4, lift-specific days | 6 sets per lift-specific muscle (2–3 sets × 2–3 sessions) + 8–12 for supporting muscles | Each main lift 2–3×/wk, 1 heavy + 1 moderate/volume day; ≥80% 1RM, 1–6 reps; powerlifting: squat 2×, bench 2–3×, deadlift 1–2× |
| Hypertrophy | 4–6 (3 with FB) | UL (4), ULPPL (5), PPL×2 (6) | 8 / 12 / 16 by level | 2×/muscle default; use 3× to keep per-session sets ≤8 direct at high volume |
| General fitness | 2–4 | FB×2–3 + 2 cardio | ≥1 hard set per major pattern on ≥2 d/wk; 6–8 per muscle fine | Prioritize adherence; meets the health floor [12] |
| Fat loss | 3–5 | FB×3 or UL×4 + steps/Z2 | Maintain intermediate targets (8–12); IF recovery poor THEN cut sets 20–30% (not frequency) | Resistance training in deficit preserves lean mass [14]; add steps/Z2 first; ≤1–2 HIIT/wk |
| Hybrid | 4–6 total (2–4 lift + 2–3 cardio) | UL×2 + 2 Z2 + 1 INT; or FB×3 + 3 cardio | 6 (strength) to 8–12 | Cluster hard days; weekly detail in [hybrid](10-hybrid.md) |

## Where cardio goes

| Lifting days | Cardio placement |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Z2 on non-lift days; INT ≤1/wk, ≥24 h from lift |
| 3 | 2 non-lifting days Z2; optional short finisher after lifting |
| 4 | Z2 on 1–2 off days; long Z2 on weekend; INT ≤1 on a non-leg day |
| 5 | One day must be rest/cardio; Z2 Wed or Sun |
| 6–7 | 1–2 easy sessions only (Z2/walk); skip INT or keep to 1 |

Constraints (canonical): same session → lift first if strength is the priority; spacing ≥6 h apart or separate days; ≥24 h before key quality sessions; lifter cardio 2–3 sessions/wk; hard intervals away from heavy leg days; Zone 2 follows upper-body days or rest days with low interference. Specific endurance duration/frequency thresholds from Wilson 2012 are unverified and omitted.

## Combining splits: rules

1. **Choose the base unit** that reaches ≥2×/muscle: U/L (4 d) or FB (3 d). Add days by layering PPL elements, specialization or cardio, not by repeating the same muscle's heavy work.
2. **5 d = U/L + one extra** (UL + Pu/arms/delts day, or full ULPPL). Count weekly sets per muscle after layering; ULPPL can give pressing muscles 3 exposures in some versions; reduce sets so the weekly total stays within the target (≤~18–20).
3. **6 d = PPL×2** or ULPPL+, with a hard rest day.
4. **No adjacent heavy lower days.** If forced, make one a light/technique day, or split quad-dominant vs hinge-dominant emphasis.
5. **Overlap check:** Push day then Upper day next day shares delts/triceps: keep ≥48 h between direct pressing sets.
6. **Max 3 consecutive hard days** before an easy/rest day.
7. **Schedule the most demanding session** (squat or deadlift) on the day after rest when possible.
8. **Missed day:** shift forward, never compress two hard sessions into consecutive days, drop the lowest-priority accessories (see [splits](06-splits.md)).
9. **Deload:** ~1 week, cut volume 30–50%, keep load mostly; every 4–8 weeks of hard training or on fatigue signals (deload survey: ≈6.4 days every ≈5.6 weeks [13], Practitioner). See [progression](08-progression.md).

## Decision rules

**Base mapping**
- IF 1 d THEN FB, 6–8 exercises, 2–3 sets each, 70–85% 1RM, 1–3 RIR; target = maintenance-to-modest growth.
- IF 2 d THEN FB A/B 3+ days apart (Mon/Thu); IF strength THEN rotate main lifts, 3–5 sets on the focus lift.
- IF 3 d THEN FB A/B/A on non-consecutive days; IF intermediate hypertrophy THEN U/L/FB.
- IF 4 d THEN UL×2; IF strength + size THEN PHUL; IF arm/leg emphasis THEN TL; IF sessions <40 min THEN FB×4.
- IF 5 d THEN ULPPL (or UL + specialization); IF hybrid THEN 3 lift + 2 cardio.
- IF 6 d THEN PPL×2 or ULPPL+; IF not advanced OR sleep <7 h THEN reduce to 5 d.
- IF 7 d THEN max 5–6 resistance days; remaining days Z2/mobility/walks.

**Frequency / volume**
- IF weekly sets for a muscle <10 THEN 1–2×/wk is fine; IF ≥10 THEN ≥2 sessions.
- IF per-session sets for any muscle >10 fractional (≈6–8 direct) THEN add a session.
- IF hypertrophy AND days ≥4 THEN 2×/muscle; IF days = 3 THEN FB×3 (≈4/session at 12/wk).
- IF strength THEN each main lift 2–3×/wk, one heavy and one moderate/volume day.
- IF beginner THEN 2–3 FB days; do not prescribe >4 days.
- IF muscle trains 3×/wk THEN limit direct sets to ≤5/session.

**Recovery / combos**
- IF two leg-dominant sessions are adjacent THEN reorder to ≥48–72 h or make one light.
- IF day N is heavy squat/deadlift AND day N+1 heavy hinge/squat THEN change N+1 to upper or light.
- IF poor sleep / high stress THEN drop one day or cut sets ~20–30%; keep frequency ≥2.
- IF performance falls 2 sessions in a row OR 5–8 weeks passed THEN deload (cut volume 30–50%, keep frequency).

**Cardio**
- IF hybrid AND lifting priority THEN lift first or ≥6 h apart / different day; INT ≤1–2/wk.
- IF power/sprint/jump goal THEN minimize high-volume running; use cycling/rowing or separate days.
- IF fat loss THEN keep lifting priority; add steps/Z2 first; no more than 1–2 HIIT/wk.
- IF time per session ≤30 min THEN FB×3–4 with 5–6 compounds, supersets of non-competing pairs.

**Goal toggles**
- IF fat loss AND recovery poor THEN keep days, cut sets/session; maintain intensity.
- IF user wants 6–7 d AND is beginner/intermediate THEN offer 4–5 d + cardio.

## Common mistakes

- Mapping 6 days to a 6-day bro split (1×/muscle).
- 5–6 days for a beginner or low-recovery user ("more is better").
- Heavy legs on consecutive days; heavy deadlift day after heavy squat day.
- 12–20 sets of one muscle in one session.
- Treating frequency as an independent growth lever while ignoring weekly sets.
- Hard intervals or long runs the day before squats/deadlifts.
- 7 hard days with no easy day; no deload.
- Counting indirect volume wrongly in ULPPL/PPL×2 (delts, triceps overloaded).
- 1-day plan with 20 exercises of 1 set instead of fewer exercises with 2–3 sets.
- Ignoring session-length limits; stacking missed sessions.
- Planning 16 sets/muscle/week into an FB×2/3 schedule (exceeds the per-session cap).

## Evidence notes

- **Frequency × hypertrophy:** 2016 meta-analysis ES 0.49 vs 0.30 favored ≥2× [1] but was not cleanly volume-equated; 2019 (25 studies) found no difference when volume equated [2]. Treat "2× beats 1× at equal volume" as Contested; ≥2× is a safe default (Strong as default, not as a proven effect).
- **Frequency × strength:** Grgic 2018 shows dose-response with frequency overall but no effect in the volume-equated subgroup (P = 0.421) [3] (Moderate). Newer Pelland analysis: strength–frequency positive with diminishing returns [15] (Moderate).
- **Trained men, 3 vs 6 sessions at equal volume:** similar adaptations [8] (Moderate, small RCT).
- **Minimum dose:** ~1 set per exercise per session, 2–3×/wk, produced significant (suboptimal) 1RM gains in trained lifters [6][7] (Moderate; abstract-level).
- **ACSM 2026** (>130 reviews): all major groups ≥2 d/wk; strength ≥80% 1RM, 2–3 sets, ≥2 sessions/wk; failure not required [9]. ACSM 2009 day staging is legacy/thin evidence (3 studies, n = 59) — present as Practitioner [16].
- **Concurrent training:** Schumann 2022 (43 studies) negligible interference for max strength (SMD −0.06) and hypertrophy (−0.01); explosive strength −0.28, only when same-session / <3 h [11]. Wilson 2012 thresholds unverified.
- **Deficit:** energy deficit impairs lean-mass gain; ~500 kcal/day threshold disputed [14].
- **Thin/unsourced:** 6–7 day frequency has no controlled trials; set-per-session ranges, calendar templates and 5–7 day structures are Practitioner synthesis. Few data on women, older adults or joint-limited populations for frequency.
